Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Does Slate sync with the iPhone Calendar and Reminders apps?
A: Yes. Slate uses Apple's own Calendar and Reminders frameworks, so any event or reminder you create in Slate appears in your native Calendar and Reminders apps, and vice versa.

Q: Are my notes backed up?
A: If you have iCloud enabled, your notes sync privately across your devices via your Apple ID. Slate does not store your notes on any external server.

Q: Does Slate require an account?
A: No. Slate does not require sign-up or a user account. It works directly with your device's Calendar, Reminders, and iCloud.

Q: I found a bug or have a feature request. What should I do?
A: Please email us at helpslateapp@163.com with details, and we'll take a look as soon as possible.
